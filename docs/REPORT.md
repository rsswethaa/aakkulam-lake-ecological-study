# Project Report — Ecological Study of Aakkulam Lake

## 1. Context
Urban lakes and wetlands can experience multiple pressures including litter, runoff, construction activity, reduced vegetation and changes in visible water condition. This project documents a structured way to examine such patterns through repeatable field observations and simple exploratory analysis.

## 2. Objective
Create a transparent research workflow that links field observations to reproducible analysis while clearly distinguishing observation, interpretation and reconstructed portfolio data.

## 3. Indicators
The portfolio framework tracks visible litter, water clarity, vegetation cover, odor, visible algae and nearby urban pressure.

## 4. Analysis
`analysis.py` groups observations by urban-pressure category and produces summary metrics. It also calculates the percentage of observations showing visible algae and generates a litter-comparison chart.

## 5. Interpretation
The illustrative dataset is constructed so that higher urban-pressure categories tend to show more litter, lower clarity and lower vegetation cover. These patterns demonstrate the analysis method only; they are not evidence about the real current ecological condition of Aakkulam Lake.

## 6. Limitations
- illustrative reconstructed dataset rather than original field measurements
- small sample size
- subjective visual scoring
- no laboratory water chemistry
- no seasonal or longitudinal coverage
- no causal inference

## 7. Future development
A stronger future study could include GPS-tagged sampling, repeated seasonal observations, dissolved oxygen/pH/turbidity measurements, GIS mapping, photographic records, statistical uncertainty and comparison against environmental standards.

## 8. Portfolio integrity
The original academic project is verified by the author's CV. This repository rebuilds the concept as a reproducible data-analysis portfolio project and does not claim that the reconstructed files are the original submission.
